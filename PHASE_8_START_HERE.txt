BOGUS BOYS 2027 — PHASE 8 (Commissioner Attendance)

1. Back up your current bb2027_v2 folder.
2. In Supabase SQL Editor, open PHASE_8_SETUP.sql in Notepad, copy its SQL, and Run.
3. In Supabase Integrations > Data API > Settings, expose public.attendance_signups, then Save.
4. Open commissioner-online.html and find Weekly Attendance & Sign-Ups.
5. Pick a week and mark an active league golfer Playing, refresh, confirm it persists.
6. Test Not Playing and No Response. No Response deletes only the signup, not the golfer.

IMPORTANT: This phase is COMMISSIONER-ENTERED attendance only. Individual golfer sign-in/sign-up is not enabled yet, because golfer roster records are not securely linked to authenticated accounts. This prevents impersonation. Guests remain managed in Guest Appearances.

This ZIP retains your existing js/supabase-config.js (the publishable key is public, but do not share your ZIP publicly). Never put a service-role/secret key in browser code.
