How this page works

Only official finalized Bogus Boys League rounds count. Handicaps use the league's nine-hole handicap calculation based on the best eight differentials from the most recent 20 qualifying rounds.

The Commissioner assigns a starting handicap until a golfer has three qualifying rounds. Quota is calculated automatically as 18 minus the rounded nine-hole handicap. Handicaps continue updating throughout the playoffs.
