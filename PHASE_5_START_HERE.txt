PHASE 5 — 2027 SEASON SCHEDULE BUILDER

1. Extract this ZIP to a NEW folder.
2. In bb2027_v2/js/supabase-config.js, enter your existing working Supabase URL and publishable key (never a secret key).
3. Open login.html and sign in. Open Online Commissioner HQ.
4. Click Add missing season weeks, then confirm.
5. The builder adds 20 regular weeks (Net, Net, Quota, Quota repeating) plus four playoff weeks, without overwriting existing records.
6. Dates remain blank. Set dates individually once your venue and start date are confirmed.
7. Playoff rounds 1-3 default to Net placeholders; playoff round 4 defaults to Low Net.

IMPORTANT: This changes live Supabase schedule records. Existing league pages are still local demo pages, not connected to the live schedule. Verify RLS policies and permissions before inviting players.
