STARTING THE POSTSEASON

Combined Re-Seeding

Regular-season best-eight BBPts determine order only. They do not carry into the playoffs. First seed starts with 1,000 points; each following seed receives 10 fewer (26th = 750).

PLAYOFF ROUND ONE

Everybody Plays 520 → 20

Round 1 placement awards 520 for first, decreasing by 20 per place through 26th. Add those awards to re-seeding points. Top 16 advance.

PLAYOFF ROUND TWO

Sweet Sixteen 1,600 → 100

Round 2 awards 1,600 for first, decreasing by 100 per place through 16th. Add these to all previous playoff points. Top 8 advance.

PLAYOFF ROUND THREE

Elite Eight 4,800 → 600

Round 3 awards 4,800 / 4,200 / 3,600 / 3,000 / 2,400 / 1,800 / 1,200 / 600. Add these to all previous playoff points. Top 4 advance.

PLAYOFF ROUND FOUR

THE FINAL FOUR

No more playoff points. The four finalists compete in straight LOW NET. Lowest net score wins the Bogus Boys Championship.

Scoring & Advancement Rules

Tied round finishes split the combined points for the positions occupied. At the advancement cutoff, golfers tied on cumulative points are separated by their lowest net score in the most recent playoff round. If still tied, the higher original playoff seed is used as a provisional fallback so the field stays at 16, 8, or 4; confirm this secondary rule before going live.

Commissioner note: All players must have a round net score entered before advancement becomes official. Changing an earlier round score clears subsequent playoff scores to prevent stale qualifiers.
