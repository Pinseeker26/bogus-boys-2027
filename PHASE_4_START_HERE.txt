BOGUS BOYS 2027 — PHASE 4: ONLINE SCHEDULE

1. Extract this ZIP to a NEW folder.
2. Copy ONLY your working project URL and sb_publishable_ key into bb2027_v2/js/supabase-config.js.
   Never enter a secret/service-role key.
3. Open bb2027_v2/login.html and sign in.
4. Click Open Online Commissioner HQ.
5. Scroll to 2027 Online Schedule. Add regular-season week 1 with a date and Net format.
6. Check that the scheduled weeks count becomes 1 and the row appears.
7. Refresh the page to confirm it persists in Supabase.

The online schedule writes to the real Supabase public.weeks table, subject to its RLS policies.
Other legacy pages still use browser demo storage and DO NOT read this schedule yet.
Do not publish legacy Commissioner HQ as a secure admin interface.
Do not enter real player information until permissions have been tested with a non-commissioner account.
