BOGUS BOYS 2027 - AUTOMATIC HANDICAP & QUOTA

1. Open commissioner.html > League Settings & Schedule. Add your actual 2027 nine-hole course/tee combinations, 9-hole rating and slope, then SAVE. No old course ratings are loaded.
2. Open handicap-history.html. Enter scores oldest to newest and select configured course/tee. Each round stores its own rating/slope snapshot.
3. Handicap = mean of best differentials among latest 20 eligible rounds, using spreadsheet count thresholds. Rounded playing handicap = nearest whole number. Quota = 18 - rounded playing handicap (no arbitrary clamp).
4. Weekly Results automatically supplies rounded handicap in Net weeks and quota target in Quota weeks. You can override the displayed value manually. On finalization, auto values are saved in the week's score record so later handicap changes do not alter locked results.
5. Historical rounds entered in earlier prototypes without saved rating/slope are intentionally NOT recalculated against the old W/N/S course constants. Re-enter them with the correct original ratings/slopes if needed.
6. Course configurations and handicap history are included in Commissioner HQ JSON backup/restore.

IMPORTANT: Browser-only prototype; not secure or shared across devices. Handicap is a custom nine-hole league method, not an official WHS index. Quota POINTS EARNED are still entered manually, and scoring points per hole are not yet automated.
